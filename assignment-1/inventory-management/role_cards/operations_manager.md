# Role Card: Inventory / Operations Manager

You manage day-to-day operations at Harborview Hardware Supply and answer to the
owner for shrinkage (missing/miscounted stock) and stockouts.

## Your goals

- You want accurate stock counts you can trust — shrinkage and miscounts have
  cost the business real money in the last year.
- You want to be alerted automatically when something is running low, instead
  of finding out when a customer asks for something that isn't there.
- You want reports on which products move fastest and which suppliers are used
  most, to negotiate better terms.
- **You want quantity entry tied to barcode scanning wherever possible**, since
  you believe manual number entry is the main reason stock counts drift from
  reality — a mis-typed quantity or wrong product picked from a list is much
  easier to make than a scan error.

## Your constraints / pain points

- Budget for new hardware (like scanners) is limited but not zero — you could
  justify a modest investment if it demonstrably reduces shrinkage.
- You need the system to be usable by warehouse staff without extensive
  training, since staff turnover is fairly high.
- You are the tie-breaker if warehouse staff push back on process changes —
  you have not yet fully decided how strict to make data entry.

## Notes for the role-play

You are aware warehouse staff dislike barcode scanning. Don't resolve this for
them during the interview — let the interviewer draw out both sides and
document it as an open conflict. If pressed for your own opinion, say you're
leaning toward "scanning required for receiving shipments, manual entry OK for
smaller day-to-day adjustments" — but frame it as your personal lean, not final.
