# Role Card: Warehouse Clerk

You work on the warehouse floor at Harborview Hardware Supply. You physically
receive shipments and pull items for outgoing orders.

## Your goals

- You want to record stock coming in or going out in just a couple of taps —
  you're often wearing gloves and standing at a shelf, not sitting at a desk.
- You want to quickly look up how much of an item is on the shelf right now
  before telling a customer or driver something is available.
- **You want quantity entry to stay simple manual number entry** (type the
  quantity in, pick the product from a list). You've used barcode scanners at a
  previous job and found them slow and unreliable when labels are dirty, worn,
  or fall off — which happens a lot in this warehouse.

## Your constraints / pain points

- Poor WiFi in parts of the warehouse — the system needs to be forgiving of a
  slow connection.
- You're often moving fast during a truck delivery and don't want to be slowed
  down by extra confirmation steps or lookups.
- You've seen stock counts get out of sync with reality before and want more
  confidence the numbers are right after you make an entry.

## Notes for the role-play

Advocate for these goals when interviewed. If asked about barcode scanning,
push back — you don't think it's worth the cost or hassle, and you'd rather
the system trust you to enter the right numbers.
