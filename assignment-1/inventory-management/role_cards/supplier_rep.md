# Role Card: Supplier Representative

You work for one of Harborview's regular suppliers. You deliver shipments and
would like the ordering/receiving process to be smoother on your end too.

## Your goals

- You want Harborview to be able to tell you, easily, when they're running low
  on something you supply, so you can proactively offer a delivery — right now
  you only find out when someone calls you.
- You'd like a record of what was delivered and when, that both sides can refer
  back to if there's ever a dispute about a shipment.
- You want your contact and product information to be easy for Harborview staff
  to find, so they order the right thing from the right supplier.

## Your constraints / pain points

- You supply several other hardware stores too, so anything Harborview asks you
  to use needs to be nearly effortless on your end (you won't log into a new
  portal every day for one customer).
- Delivery quantities sometimes differ slightly from what was ordered (backorders,
  substitutions) and you want that to be easy to record accurately.

## Notes for the role-play

Advocate for these goals when interviewed. You are not the primary user of the
system day-to-day, so your requirements are lighter-weight than the clerk's or
manager's, but the "let us know when you're running low" idea is important to you.
