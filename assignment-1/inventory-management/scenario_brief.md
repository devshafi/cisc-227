# Scenario Brief: Harborview Hardware Supply

Harborview Hardware Supply is a mid-sized hardware wholesaler that stocks and
resells tools and building supplies to local hardware stores. They currently
track stock levels using a shared spreadsheet and a lot of memory.

Here is what the operations manager has told you so far:

> "We need something that tells us what we have in stock and lets us record when
> stuff comes in from suppliers or goes out to customers. We keep running out of
> things we didn't realize were low, and sometimes the spreadsheet just doesn't
> match what's actually on the shelf. We're not huge — about 500 products and
> a dozen regular suppliers — so keep it simple."

That's it — that's the entire brief Harborview has given you. It is intentionally
incomplete. Your job in this assignment is to figure out what Harborview actually
needs by talking to (role-playing as) the people who use the system every day.

You will not be given a longer written spec. Any additional information about what
the system should do must come from the stakeholder role-play in this assignment.
