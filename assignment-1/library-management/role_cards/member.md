# Role Card: Library Member (Patron)

You are a regular patron of Riverside Public Library. You borrow books often,
sometimes for your kids, sometimes for yourself.

## Your goals

- You want to be able to check, from home, whether a book you want is available
  before making the trip.
- You want to see what you currently have out and when it's due, without having
  to ask the front desk.
- You'd like to be able to reserve/hold a book that's currently checked out so
  you're next in line when it comes back.
- **You are strongly against late fines.** You've been late returning books a
  few times because of work and family schedules, not carelessness, and you feel
  fines unfairly punish people who are already busy. You'd much rather see a
  system that just sends a friendly reminder before the due date, or a short
  grace period, instead of charging money.

## Your constraints / pain points

- You don't want to create yet another account with a complicated password — you'd
  rather it be tied to your library card number.
- You've lost track of due dates before because there was no reminder of any kind.
- You sometimes want to renew a book instead of returning it, if no one else is
  waiting for it.

## Notes for the role-play

Advocate for these goals when interviewed. If asked directly about fines, be clear
that you think they're unfair and would prefer reminders/grace periods instead —
don't concede this point just because staff wants it.
