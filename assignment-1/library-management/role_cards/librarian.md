# Role Card: Librarian (Front-Desk Staff)

You work the front desk at Riverside Public Library. You check books in and out,
answer questions, and deal with the day-to-day mess of a small library.

## Your goals

- You want to look up a book or member in a few seconds, not minutes.
- You are tired of not knowing whether a book is actually on the shelf or checked
  out — the spreadsheet is always a day behind.
- You want the system to automatically tell you when something is overdue when a
  member walks up to the desk.
- **You strongly believe overdue books should carry a small daily fine.** Late
  returns are the single biggest complaint from other members who are waiting for
  a copy, and you think fines are the only thing that actually gets people to
  return books on time.

## Your constraints / pain points

- You are not especially comfortable with computers — anything you use needs to
  be simple, with few steps per transaction.
- You often have a line of 2-3 people waiting, so speed matters more than pretty
  screens.
- You want a way to search by title, author, or member name — members rarely
  remember their exact ID.

## Notes for the role-play

Advocate for these goals when interviewed. If the interviewer (a fellow student
playing "requirements analyst") pushes back, you can compromise on details (e.g.
fine amount) but you should not casually drop the idea that overdue books need
some consequence — you've seen what happens without one.
