# Scenario Brief: Riverside Public Library

Riverside Public Library currently tracks its books and lending using a mix of a
paper card catalog and a shared spreadsheet. The head librarian has asked your team
to design a small software system to replace this setup.

Here is what the library has told you so far:

> "We want something that keeps track of our books and who has borrowed them. Staff
> should be able to look things up quickly, and we want to reduce the number of books
> that go missing or come back late. We don't have a big budget, so keep it simple —
> we're a small branch with about 3,000 books and 800 active members."

That's it — that's the entire brief the library has given you. It is intentionally
incomplete. Your job in this assignment is to figure out what the library actually
needs by talking to (role-playing as) the people who will use the system every day.

You will not be given a longer written spec. Any additional information about what
the system should do must come from the stakeholder role-play in this assignment.
