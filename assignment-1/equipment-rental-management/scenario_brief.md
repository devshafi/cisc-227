# Scenario Brief: Trailside Outdoor Gear Rental

Trailside Outdoor Gear Rental rents camping and outdoor equipment — tents,
kayaks, bikes, and similar gear — to weekend visitors near a popular trailhead.
They currently track rentals with a paper logbook and a whiteboard.

Here is what the shop owner has told you so far:

> "We need something that tracks what gear we have, who's renting what, and
> when it's due back. We're losing track of things during busy weekends, and
> some gear has gone missing or come back damaged without us really knowing
> who had it last. We're a small shop — about 150 pieces of gear and maybe
> 50 rentals on a busy weekend — so keep it simple."

That's it — that's the entire brief Trailside has given you. It is
intentionally incomplete. Your job in this assignment is to figure out what
Trailside actually needs by talking to (role-playing as) the people who use
the system every day.

You will not be given a longer written spec. Any additional information about
what the system should do must come from the stakeholder role-play in this
assignment.
