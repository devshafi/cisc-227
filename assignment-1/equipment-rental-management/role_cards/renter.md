# Role Card: Renter (Customer)

You're a regular visitor to the trailhead and rent gear from Trailside a few
times a season — sometimes a tent for an overnight trip, sometimes just a
bike for the afternoon.

## Your goals

- You want to check, before driving out, whether the gear you want is
  actually available that day.
- You want a fast checkout — you're usually eager to get on the trail, not
  stand at a counter filling out forms.
- You'd like a simple record of what you have out and when it's due back, so
  you don't accidentally keep something too long.
- **You are strongly against deposit holds.** You've rented from Trailside for
  years without ever damaging anything, and you feel like a deposit treats
  every customer as a suspect. You'd much rather they just take your name and
  trust you to bring things back in good shape, the way it's always worked.

## Your constraints / pain points

- You don't want to create an account with a password just to rent a tent for
  one weekend — you'd rather it just be your name and phone number.
- You've been surprised by a due date before because nobody mentioned it
  clearly at checkout.
- Sometimes you want to extend a rental by a day or two if you're not ready to
  bring gear back yet.

## Notes for the role-play

Advocate for these goals when interviewed. If asked directly about deposits,
be clear you think they're unnecessary and unfair to regular customers —
don't concede this point just because staff wants it.
