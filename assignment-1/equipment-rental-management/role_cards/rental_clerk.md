# Role Card: Rental Clerk (Front-Desk Staff)

You work the counter at Trailside Outdoor Gear Rental. You check gear out and
in, and deal with the chaos of a busy weekend morning.

## Your goals

- You want to look up whether a piece of gear is available in a few seconds,
  not minutes — Saturday mornings get a line out the door.
- You want to know instantly who currently has a given piece of gear, since
  you're the one who gets blamed when something comes back damaged and nobody
  can say who had it.
- **You strongly believe renters should show ID and put down a deposit hold
  before taking out higher-value gear** (kayaks, bikes) — you've seen gear
  "lost" or badly damaged too many times, and a deposit is the only thing
  that's made renters more careful in your experience.

## Your constraints / pain points

- You're often juggling a line of customers, so anything you use needs to be
  fast — a couple of taps or fields, not a long form.
- You want to search by renter name or gear name, since people rarely
  remember exact ID numbers.
- You'd like a way to flag a piece of gear as "out of service" when it comes
  back damaged, so it doesn't get rented out again by accident before it's
  fixed.

## Notes for the role-play

Advocate for these goals when interviewed. If the interviewer pushes back on
the deposit idea, you can compromise on specifics (e.g. which gear needs a
deposit, how much) but don't casually drop the idea that some accountability
step is needed for expensive gear — you've seen what happens without one.
